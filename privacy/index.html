Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: your route stays on your phone. We keep the areas you uncover so your map survives a new phone. You decide what friends or anyone else can see. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. Who we are

Xplrearth (“we”) is an app made by an independent developer based in the European Union. We are responsible for your personal data in Xplrearth. Contact: savvassa18@outlook.com.

2. What we collect and why

Location

  • Your location is processed on your phone to clear the fog where you have been. If you allow “Allow all the time”, this also happens while the app is closed.
  • Raw location points stay on your phone and are deleted within 24 hours.
  • We back up to your account only the explored areas (squares of about 120 m), daily distances by type (walking, cycling, driving), time moving, and totals per country. We never store your route.
  • Approximate live location: only when you start sharing with friends, we store a square of about 500 m (your latest one only). Sharing ends automatically within 24 hours, and never happens at home or in your privacy zones.
  • Your home and privacy zones are kept on your phone only.

Account and profile

Your email address (or your Google or Apple sign-in), display name, @handle, and optionally a bio, profile photo, banner and featured badge.

Friends and activity

Friend requests, friends and blocks; notifications; badges, levels and challenge progress; whether you joined the public leaderboard.

Photos

  • With your permission, the app reads where photos in your library were taken, on your phone only, to show them on your private map. Your photo library is not uploaded.
  • Photos you choose to share are uploaded as a smaller copy with all hidden information (EXIF, GPS) removed, plus your caption, a place name and an area of about 500 m.
  • Ratings you give and reports you make.

Trip planner

The destination and departure city you choose, travel dates, trip length, number of travellers and interests, to find ideas and prices. Trips you save are stored in your account. Your location is not sent; the departure city is only pre-filled on your phone, and you can change it.

Technical data

Crash reports (with location and tokens removed) to fix bugs. To show the map, the map provider receives the map area you look at and your IP address.

3. Who can see what

Only youYour map, stats (unless you share them), trips, photo collection, privacy zones
Your friendsThe stats you choose to share; your approximate live location while you share it; photos you share with friends
Anyone signed inYour name, @handle, photo and bio if you can be found in search; photos you share publicly; your new area on the public leaderboard if you join it

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract: your account, map backup and the features you use.
  • Consent: background location, photo library access, live location sharing, notifications and automatic photo detection. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or your phone settings.
  • Legitimate interest: security, preventing abuse, crash reports, and checking shared photos to keep the community safe.

6. International transfers

Your data is stored in the EU. Some providers above (Mapbox, Anthropic, Google, Travelpayouts) may process data outside the EU. Where they do, they are bound by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep data

  • Raw location points: on your phone only, up to 24 hours
  • Approximate live location: until sharing ends (at most 24 hours)
  • Notifications: 90 days
  • Photos refused by the automatic check: deleted immediately
  • Everything else: until you delete it or your account

8. Your rights

  • Export: Profile → Settings → Your data → Export my data gives you a file with everything we store about you.
  • Delete: Profile → Settings → Your data → Delete account removes everything immediately. Without the app, use the account deletion page.
  • Correct: edit your profile any time.
  • Object or withdraw consent: use the switches in Settings or your phone’s permission settings.
  • You can also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Children

Xplrearth is for people aged 16 and over.

10. Security

All connections are encrypted. Every database table only lets you read your own data, or what others chose to share with you. Raw GPS never reaches our servers. Shared photos are stored privately and shown through short-lived links.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
